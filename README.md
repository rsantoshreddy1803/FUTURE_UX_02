# LUMÉ — Mobile Salon Appointment Booking App

A mobile-first salon appointment booking experience designed as part of **Task 2 of the Future Interns UI/UX Design Internship Program**.

LUMÉ focuses on making salon appointment booking simple, transparent, and frictionless for both first-time and returning customers.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Traditional salon bookings often rely on phone calls and WhatsApp messages, which can lead to unclear service information, scheduling confusion, and difficulty managing appointments.

LUMÉ addresses these challenges through a structured mobile booking experience where users can:

- Explore salon services
- View service pricing and duration
- Select a preferred stylist
- Choose a date and time
- Confirm appointments
- View upcoming bookings
- Receive appointment reminders
- Quickly rebook previous services

---

## 🎯 Objective

To design a professional mobile appointment booking experience that:

- Reduces booking friction
- Makes service information easy to understand
- Provides a clear and predictable booking flow
- Helps users manage upcoming appointments
- Makes repeat bookings faster and easier

---

## 🔍 Research

### Secondary Research

Reviewed common salon appointment booking patterns and existing digital booking experiences to understand recurring customer needs and usability challenges.

### Competitive Analysis

Analyzed existing booking experiences focusing on:

- Service information
- Pricing and duration
- Booking flow
- Availability
- Confirmation
- Appointment management
- Repeat bookings

### Key Insights

- Customers need clear service information before booking.
- Booking should require minimal effort.
- Users need clear confirmation after booking.
- Upcoming appointments should be easy to access.
- Returning customers benefit from quick rebooking.
